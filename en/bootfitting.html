      Bootfitting

      No more sore feet

      The right boot should feel right from the first turn. We analyse your foot, choose a boot to match and adjust it until it fits.

      book bootfitting

      Why

      Every foot is different

      A boot that pinches ruins the day, and a boot that is loose takes away control. Between the two is a fit that lets you forget your feet and think about your skiing.

      That goes for racers as much as for people who ski a few days a year. We have worked with skis in the Åre valley since 2004, and the workshop has always been the heart of the store.

      How it works

      Six steps to the right fit

      1. Step 1 / 6

        Foot analysis

        It starts with a short conversation about your skiing, your needs and your goals. Then we map your foot: arch height, foot width and the mobility of your foot and lower leg, and look for problem areas that need extra attention.

      2. Step 2 / 6

        Boot position

        Your basic stance is the position that suits your body, without awkward angles that disrupt your skiing. When the foot no longer has to tense up to fit the boot, you get better control and power transfer.

      3. Step 3 / 6

        Trying and choosing

        Based on your foot profile and goals we bring out models that match. You try them on and feel the fit. We choose by your foot, regardless of brand or flex.

      4. Step 4 / 6

        Custom insoles

        A moulded insole is the foundation, whether you want maximum comfort or maximum power to the ski. It places the foot in a neutral position so you stand stable.

      5. Step 5 / 6

        Grinding and stretching

        We heat liners, grind out the shell and build up around the heel, last, flex and toe box. Sometimes heating is enough, sometimes more is needed. We may adjust several times after you have been skiing, and we are happy to.

      6. Step 6 / 6

        Foam-injected liner

        For anyone who wants a boot that fits like a cast. We inject PU foam into the liner while you wear it, so it moulds exactly to your foot.
