        Training

        Modern ski technique

        For experienced skiers who want to go deeper into technique and equipment. Each step runs three days with skiing 09:00–15:00 and evening theory and workshops in the store. We arrange transport to and from the slopes. Price 4,500 SEK per step.

        • Step 1, 15–18 January 2027. Balance, movement patterns and the difference between skidded, steered and carved turns.
        • Step 2, 5–7 February 2027. Edge angles, body position, varied terrain and easier off-piste. Boot theory with bootfitting.
        • Step 3, 12–14 February 2027. Controlled free skiing in varied terrain and snow, off-piste and avalanche safety.

        FAQ

        Good to know

        Cannot find the answer? Call +46 647 305 00.

        Where do we collect the gear?

        At the store on Fjällfararvägen 10 in Undersåker or at our service station at Hotel Fjällgården, right by the slopes.

        Can we swap boots during the week?

        Yes. If the boot does not fit, you can swap it during the week.

        Is a helmet included?

        For ages 0–17 a helmet is included in the alpine and snowboard packages. Adults can rent a helmet separately.

        What is included for longer rentals?

        If you rent for more than four days, an extra grind, wax or ski swap is included.

        Does it cost to change equipment?

        Changes carry an admin fee of 70 SEK, plus any price difference.

        Do groups get a discount?

        Yes, from more than 10 people if you book at least five days before arrival. See group discounts in the price list.

        Can you fit boots we bought elsewhere?

        Yes. Come in and we will assess what is needed. The price depends on the extent of the work.

        Where does the ski coach meet us?

        At your cabin, hotel or on the mountain, whichever you prefer.

        Can offers be combined?

        No. Offers apply to rental, not purchases, and cannot be combined.

        About us

        From joinery to specialist store

        It started in 2004 in an old joinery workshop, with a few pairs of skis, a grinding machine and four friends who skied the valley a lot and wanted to rent really good skis to Åre's visitors. Back then the business was called Hyrskidan.

        Daniel joined in 2006, and the focus became one thing: giving every customer their perfect ski day. The year after, we took over the petrol station in Undersåker. The space gave us room to grow, and the ski workshop became the heart of a store that wanted to be more than a rental shop.

        In 2018 Daniel started the coaching business that is now Åre Ski Academy. One ski instructor has become a team of 20 coaches, and Hyrskidan has become Åre Sportshop.

        We know skis, and so much more.
